# Meeting follow-up

After almost every client or internal call I send a short follow-up: what we decided, who does what by when, and anything still open.

## Before you write

1. Read the newest good example, `processes/meeting-follow-up/examples/good-01.md`, and match its structure, length and tone.
2. Read `processes/meeting-follow-up/corrections.md`. Corrections override everything else, including the example.
3. Take tone and constraints from `profile.md`. For the people, clients, goals and terms the task involves, call `loam_get_context` with the task text.

## Steps

1. Take my raw notes from the call.
2. List decisions, then actions with an owner and a date.
3. Add anything still open and who will answer it.
4. Send within an hour of the call.

## What a good result looks like

Five to eight lines. Each action has a first name and a date. Nothing in it is a surprise to anyone who was on the call.

## Constraints

Only what was said on the call. No new commitments. Never send a client the internal part of a discussion.

## Rules from corrections (newest first)

1. Owners by first name only, and put each deadline in bold so it can be found at a glance. Was: "Action for the Northlight team: send the revised plan by next week.". Should be: "Marco: send the revised plan by **Thursday 8 October**.".
