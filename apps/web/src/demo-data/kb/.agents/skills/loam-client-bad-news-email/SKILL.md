# Client bad-news email

Use this when the user has to tell a client about a delay, a miss or a problem.

## Before you write

- Read `profile.md` for the user's tone and how they sign off.
- Look the client up in `context/entities.md`. If it says how they like to be addressed or what is sensitive for them, follow it.
- If you do not know what happened, what the new date is or what is being done about it, ask the user. Do not fill gaps with guesses.

## Structure: four short paragraphs

1. **The fact.** Say what is late or wrong in the first sentence. No warm-up.
2. **The reason, without excuses.** One or two sentences about what happened, not about who is to blame.
3. **What we are doing.** The concrete steps already under way, and what the client needs to do, if anything.
4. **When they hear from us next.** A specific day for the next update and what it will contain.

## Rules

- The subject line states the news plainly, for example "Delay: [deliverable] now [new date]".
- Avoid "unfortunately" and "we apologise for any inconvenience". One honest apology sentence at most, placed where it belongs.
- Do not promise a new date the user has not given you.
- Keep it under 150 words.
- This is a draft. The user reads it and sends it themselves.

Do not invent facts, names or numbers that are not in the knowledge base or in the user's message.
