# Glossary

Terms and abbreviations that would confuse an outsider.

- **ROAS** — return on ad spend: revenue divided by what we spent on ads. A ROAS of 4 means four euros back for every euro spent.
- **CPM** — cost per thousand impressions. What it costs to show an ad 1,000 times.
- **CPC** — cost per click. Total spend divided by clicks.
- **CTR** — click-through rate: the share of people who saw an ad and clicked it.
- **MQL** — marketing-qualified lead: someone who has shown enough interest to be passed to sales.
- **SOW** — statement of work: the document that says exactly what we will deliver, by when, for how much.
- **Retainer** — a fixed monthly fee for an agreed amount of work, rather than paying per project.
- **QBR** — quarterly business review: the longer call with a client each quarter to look at results and plan ahead.
- **Pacing** — whether a campaign is on track to spend its monthly budget evenly, not too fast or too slow.
- **Creative brief** — the one-page document that tells the creative team what to make, for whom, and why.
