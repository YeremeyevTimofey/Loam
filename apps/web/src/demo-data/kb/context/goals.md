# Goals

What you are working towards or must get done, with the date if there is one. An assistant uses these to judge what matters most. One per line, like this: `- **Keep the three biggest accounts** (goal, by 2026-12-31) — the spring budget is the risk.`

- **Keep the three biggest accounts** (goal) — Acme Outdoor, Brightside Dental and Fernwood Foods; the spring campaign budget is the sensitive point at Acme.
- **Every weekly report out on Friday** (goal) — no late reports; Lena sees anything new before it goes to the client.
- **Spend less time on the weekly report** (goal) — it takes about 40 minutes per client now.
