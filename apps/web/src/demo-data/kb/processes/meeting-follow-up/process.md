# Meeting follow-up

## What we do

After almost every client or internal call I send a short follow-up: what we decided, who does what by when, and anything still open.

## Steps

1. Take my raw notes from the call.
2. List decisions, then actions with an owner and a date.
3. Add anything still open and who will answer it.
4. Send within an hour of the call.

## What a good result looks like

Five to eight lines. Each action has a first name and a date. Nothing in it is a surprise to anyone who was on the call.

## Constraints

Only what was said on the call. No new commitments. Never send a client the internal part of a discussion.

## What it takes

Just my notes. A quick human read before sending, because the email goes out in my name. Low risk.
