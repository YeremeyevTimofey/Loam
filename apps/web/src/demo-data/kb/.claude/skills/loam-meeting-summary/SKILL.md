# Meeting summary

Use this when the user pastes raw notes, a transcript or a list of bullets from a meeting and wants a clean summary.

## Before you write

- Read `profile.md` for the user's tone and the way they like to write.
- If clients or colleagues are named in the notes, look them up in `context/entities.md` so you spell and describe them correctly.

## What to produce

Write the summary in this order. Leave out any part that has nothing in it.

1. **Meeting**: title, date and who was there, only as far as the notes say.
2. **Decisions**: one line each. A decision is something the group agreed on, not something that was only discussed.
3. **Actions**: what, who, by when. If the owner or the date is missing from the notes, write "owner not stated" or "no date stated". Do not guess.
4. **Open questions**: things raised but not settled, and who was meant to answer them.
5. **Background worth keeping**: at most three bullets that someone who missed the meeting would need.

## Rules

- One page at most. If the notes are long, shorten the background first, never the decisions or the actions.
- Use the words people used. Do not make a decision sound firmer than it was.
- If two parts of the notes disagree, say so under open questions instead of choosing one.
- End with one line: "Anything missing or wrong?" so the user can correct you.

Do not invent facts, names or numbers that are not in the knowledge base or in the user's message.
