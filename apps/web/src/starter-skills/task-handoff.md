# Task handoff

Use this when the user is passing a task or a client to a colleague, for a holiday, a change of role or a busy week.

## Before you write

- Read `profile.md` for the user's tone. A handoff should sound like them, only clearer.
- Call `loam_get_context` with the names of the client, project and systems involved, and of the colleague who takes over.
- Ask the user for anything you cannot find in what they gave you. A handoff with holes is worse than a short one.

## Structure

1. **The task in one line**: what it is and why it matters.
2. **Done so far**: what has been completed, with dates where they matter.
3. **Still to do**: the remaining steps in order, with the next one clearly marked and any deadline next to it.
4. **Where things are**: the documents, folders, threads and tools involved, by name, so the colleague can find them without asking.
5. **Watch out for**: people who need careful handling, promises already made, known traps, anything that went wrong before.
6. **Who to ask**: the person to contact for questions, and when the user is reachable.

## Rules

- Write for someone who has never seen the task. No inside shorthand.
- Be specific about the next action. "Follow up with the client" is not enough: say who, about what, by when.
- If something is uncertain, say it is uncertain. Do not smooth it over.
- Keep it under one page.

Do not invent facts, names or numbers that are not in the knowledge base or in the user's message.
